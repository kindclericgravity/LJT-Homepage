Welcome to my academic homepage! I am a Ph.D. student at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Professor Junxian He in the HKUST NLP Group. My research interests lie in natural language processing, machine learning, large language model reasoning, and hallucination mitigation in vision-language models.

## About Me

I am currently a first-year Ph.D. student at HKUST, working on various aspects of natural language processing and machine learning. Before starting my doctoral studies, I received my Bachelor's degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I was advised by Professor Junxian He.

I have research intern experiences at MINIMAX (February 2025 - Present), Tencent WXG (June 2024 - September 2024), and Shanghai AI Lab (June 2023 - December 2023). During these internships, I have worked on topics including LLM reasoning, hallucination in vision-language models, and LLM truthfulness.

## Research

My research interests include LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning, Hallucination in Vision-Language Models, and LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability.
